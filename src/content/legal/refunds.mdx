This policy sets out our **standard** terms for cancelling a {site.name} subscription and getting a refund. If your school has a signed agreement with {site.company.legalName} that says something different, **the signed agreement applies instead**.

## Billing

- Subscriptions are billed **yearly in advance**, or on another schedule agreed in your order form.
- Prices exclude GST, which is shown separately on the invoice.

## Cancelling

- You can **cancel at any time** by emailing {site.company.email} from your school's registered email address.
- After you cancel, your school keeps full access **until the end of the period you have already paid for**. The subscription then ends and is not renewed.
- After the subscription ends, we keep your school's data for **90 days** so you can ask for an export, and then delete it, as described in our [Privacy policy](/privacy).

"A new subscription" means your school's first paid subscription with us, not a renewal.

**Example:** a school pays for 12 months on 1 June and cancels on 20 June. Eleven full months (July to May) are unused, so it receives 11/12 of the fee it paid.

## Duplicate or wrong payments

If you notice a duplicate or wrong payment, email us with the payment details. Once we confirm it, we refund the amount **within 7–10 working days** to the **original payment method**. Your bank may take a few more days to show the money in your account.

## How to request a refund

Email {site.company.email} with:

- your school's name,
- the invoice number and payment date,
- the reason for the request.

We will confirm the amount and the date you can expect the refund.
