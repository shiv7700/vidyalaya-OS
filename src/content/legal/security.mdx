Schools trust {site.name} with records about children, families and staff. This page describes how the app protects that data today. It lists only measures that are built into the product; if something is not on this page, please do not assume we do it — ask us at {site.company.email}.

## Each school's data is kept separate

- Every record that belongs to a school — students, attendance, marks, fees, everything — carries that school's ID.
- Every database query filters by the school of the **logged-in user**. The school is taken from the login session, never from what the browser sends, so a user cannot ask for another school's data by changing a request.
- Asking for a record that belongs to another school gets a "not found" answer, exactly as if it did not exist.
- Parents can reach only the children linked to their own login, and students only their own records.
- Automated tests check these rules: another school's records, a wrong role, and a parent opening someone else's child are all refused.

## Role-based access

- Each person has one role: school admin, teacher, student or parent.
- Every part of the server states which roles may use it, and the server checks this on **every request**. Hiding a button in the screen is never the only protection.
- The user is looked up again on every request, so turning off an account or changing a role takes effect immediately.

## Logins and passwords

- **Passwords are never stored in readable form.** They are hashed with **scrypt**, a deliberately slow algorithm, using a unique random salt for each password, and compared in constant time.
- **Account lockout:** after **10 wrong passwords**, an account is locked for **15 minutes**.
- **Rate limits:** login attempts are limited per network address and login name, and the public admission enquiry and demo request forms are rate-limited and include a hidden field that catches automated spam. All other requests are also rate-limited.
- **Passwords set by staff** — for a new account or after a reset — must be changed by the person at their next login.
- Staff log in with their email address, parents with their mobile number, and students with a username given by the school.

## Sessions

- A login creates **one cookie**, which is:
  - `httpOnly` — scripts on the page cannot read it, which protects it from being stolen through injected code;
  - `SameSite=Lax` — it is not sent with requests started by other websites;
  - `Secure` in production — it is sent only over HTTPS.
- The session token is cryptographically signed, and the server refuses to start in production without a strong signing secret.
- A session lasts at most 7 days, and **ends immediately** when:
  - the person logs out (this logs them out on all devices);
  - their password is changed or reset;
  - their account is turned off, for example when a teacher or student leaves;
  - a parent's last linked child is unlinked;
  - the school's account is paused.

## Security headers

- The server that answers the app's requests sends headers that stop browsers from guessing content types (`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`), forbid other sites from framing it (`X-Frame-Options: DENY` and a Content Security Policy with `frame-ancestors 'none'`), send no referrer information, and do not advertise the server software.
- In production it also tells browsers to use HTTPS only (Strict Transport Security, for one year).
- This website sends similar headers and blocks access to the camera, microphone and location.
- The app's server does not allow cross-origin requests, so another website cannot use a visitor's browser to read data from it.

## Every request is checked

- Every request is validated against a strict definition of what it may contain — types, lengths, formats, allowed values. **Unknown fields are rejected**, not ignored.
- Oversized requests are rejected.
- Text that people type, such as notices and notes, is always shown as text: HTML or scripts typed into a form are never run.
- Error messages do not reveal internal details of the system.

## Money and records stay correct

- Amounts are stored as whole paise, so there are no rounding errors in fee totals.
- **A fee receipt can be voided, but never deleted.** Voiding records the reason, who voided it and when; the receipt number stays used and the receipt shows as VOID. A fee bill that has payments cannot be removed.
- **No double payments:** while a payment is being checked and saved, that fee is locked in the database, so two payments recorded at the same moment cannot both pass the balance check. A payment can never exceed what is due.
- The same kind of locking stops a library lending more copies than it has, a hostel room going over capacity, and an enquiry being admitted twice.
- Receipt and certificate numbers are unique within each school, enforced by the database.
- The app records who marked attendance, entered marks, collected or voided a payment, issued a book and issued a certificate. (This is not a full audit log of every change.)

## What schools can do

- Give each person their own login, and never share passwords.
- Turn off the accounts of staff and students who leave — the app ends their sessions at once.
- Reset a password straight away if you think it is known to someone else.
- Record only what you need, especially in free-text notes and remarks.
