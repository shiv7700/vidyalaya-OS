Teachers apply for leave in Vidyalaya OS, and the school admin approves or rejects it. Payroll then makes each month's payslips, taking off approved unpaid leave on its own.

## Leave

### Leave types

- **Casual**
- **Sick**
- **Earned**
- **Unpaid**: deducted from salary in payroll

There are no yearly leave quotas in the app. Each teacher's **Leave** page shows how many days of each type they have taken this year.

### Applying for leave (teacher)

1. Click **Leave** in the sidebar (under Me), then **Apply for leave**.
2. Choose the **Type**.
3. Pick the **From** and **To** dates. For one day, use the same date in both.
4. Give a **Reason**.
5. Click **Apply**.

The request shows as pending until the admin decides. While it's pending, you can **Cancel** it.

### Leave rules

- **Sundays and school holidays aren't counted.** A Saturday-to-Monday leave is 2 days.
- A request can't be only Sundays or holidays.
- One request covers **at most 60 days**. Split longer leave into more requests.
- Leave must be in this school year or the next (April to March).
- You can't apply for days you already have leave on.

### Approving leave (school admin)

1. Go to **Leave requests** (under Staff). It opens on **Pending**.
2. Check the teacher, type, days, dates and reason.
3. Click **Approve** or **Reject** and confirm.

A decision **can't be changed later**, so check first. Use the **Show** list to see approved, rejected or all requests; each shows who decided it. Pending requests appear on your **Dashboard** under **Needs attention**.

## Payroll

Payroll covers teachers and school admins.

### Step 1: Set each person's pay

1. Go to **Payroll** (under Finance). Scroll to **Staff pay**.
2. Click **Set pay** (or **Change**) next to a person.
3. Enter **Basic** and **Allowances**, both in rupees per month.
4. Click **Save**.

New payslips use these amounts. Changing pay doesn't alter payslips already made.

### Step 2: Make the month's payslips

1. Pick the **Month** at the top.
2. Click **Make payslips**.

A draft payslip is made for everyone with pay set. People who already have a payslip for that month are skipped, so it's safe to click again after adding someone. You can't make payslips for a future month.

**Unpaid leave** that was approved for that month is deducted automatically: gross pay divided by the number of days in the month, times the unpaid leave days in that month. Sundays and school holidays inside the leave aren't counted, the same as when the leave was applied for.

### Step 3: Adjust deductions

On a **Draft** payslip, click **Deductions**. Enter the **total** deductions: unpaid leave, PF, advances and anything else taken off. The app shows the net pay as you type. Deductions can't be more than the gross pay.

### Step 4: Mark as paid

Once the salary has gone out, click **Mark paid**, check the **Paid on** date and confirm. **Paid payslips can't be changed.**

The table shows gross, unpaid days, deductions, net and status for everyone. At the top you see the month's totals: gross, net to pay and paid.

### Printing payslips

Click **View** on any payslip, then **Print**. It has your school letterhead, the month, basic, allowances, gross, deductions (with unpaid leave days) and net pay, plus signature lines.

## What teachers see

- **Leave:** their requests, status and days taken by type.
- **Payslips:** their **paid** payslips, month by month, each with **View** and **Print**. Drafts don't show until they're marked paid.
- **Today** page: a note when a leave request is waiting for approval.
