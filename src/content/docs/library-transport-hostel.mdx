These three pages sit under **Facilities** in the school admin's sidebar. Use the ones your school needs and ignore the rest.

## Library

### Add books

1. Go to **Library** and click **Add book**.
2. Enter the **Title** and **Author**. **Category** (for example "Fiction") and **ISBN** are optional.
3. Enter the number of **Copies**.
4. Click **Save**.

The list shows each title with how many copies are free, for example "3 of 5". Search by title, author, ISBN or category.

### Issue a book

1. Click **Issue** on the book. It's greyed out when no copies are free.
2. Find the borrower by name or class (for example "5-A"). You can lend to students and teachers.
3. Check **Due back on**. It's two weeks from today unless you change it.
4. Click **Issue book**.

You can't lend more copies than the library has.

### Returns and overdue books

Go to **Book loans**. Switch between **Out now**, **Overdue** and **Returned**. When a book comes back, click **Returned** and confirm.

Overdue books also appear on your **Dashboard**. Students and parents see the child's books in the **Library** tab, with the due date, and a reminder at the top of their page when a book is due within 3 days or overdue.

There are no library fines in the app.

### Edit or remove a book

Click **Edit** to change details or the number of copies. You can't set fewer copies than are out on loan. A book that has never been lent can be deleted; otherwise its history stays, so set its copies to 0 instead.

## Transport

### Add a route

1. Go to **Transport** and click **Add route**.
2. Enter the **Route name** (for example "Route 1 – Sector 21"), **Vehicle number**, **Driver** and **Driver's mobile**.
3. Enter the **Monthly fee (₹)**, or leave it empty if there's no transport fee.
4. Click **Save**.

### Add stops and students

Click **Stops & riders** on a route.

1. Under **Stops**, type the **New stop** and its **Pickup** time, then click **Add**. Stops are listed by pickup time.
2. Click **Add student**, find the student, choose where they **Get on at**, and click **Add**.

A student can be on one route only. Adding them to another route moves them. Click **Remove** to take a student off the bus; fees already billed stay.

You can't remove a stop while students get on there, or delete a route while students ride it.

### Print the rider list

Click **Print list**. It lists riders stop by stop, with their class, roll number and parent's name and login mobile. Give a copy to the driver or conductor.

### Bill the monthly transport fee

On the route's page, under **Monthly fee**, pick the **Month** and click **Bill**. Each rider gets a fee called, for example, "Transport – Sept 2026" in [Fees](/docs/fees). Students already billed for that month are skipped. The fee is due on the 10th of the month, or a week from today if that's later.

Students and parents see the route, stop, pickup time, vehicle and driver's number in the **Bus & hostel** tab.

## Hostel

### Add a hostel and rooms

1. Go to **Hostel** and click **Add hostel**.
2. Enter the **Hostel name**, **Warden** and **Warden's mobile**, and the **Monthly fee (₹)** (leave empty for none).
3. Click **Save**.
4. Click **Add room**, enter the **Room number** and the number of **Beds** (1 to 50), and click **Save**.

Each room shows beds used, like "2/3".

### Allot beds

1. Click **Allot bed** on the hostel.
2. Find the student and pick a **Room** with a free bed.
3. Click **Allot**.

A room can never hold more students than its beds. A student already in another room is moved. To free a bed, click the **×** next to the student's name and confirm **Move out**; hostel fees already billed stay.

### Bill the monthly hostel fee

Under the hostel, pick a month in **Bill hostel fee for** and click **Bill residents**. It works like transport billing: one fee per resident per month, due on the 10th or a week from today.

A room or hostel can be deleted only when nobody lives in it.

Students and parents see the hostel, room, date they moved in, and the warden's name and number in the **Bus & hostel** tab.

## When a student leaves

Marking a student as left takes them off their bus route and out of their hostel room automatically. See [Year end and student changes](/docs/year-end).
