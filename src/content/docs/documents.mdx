Vidyalaya OS prints student ID cards for a whole section at once, and issues numbered bonafide and transfer certificates on your letterhead. You'll find both under **Documents** in the school admin's sidebar.

## First, check your letterhead

Certificates (and report cards, receipts and payslips) carry your school's name, address, phone and email at the top. Fill these in once under **School details**. To change the school's name itself, contact the Vidyalaya OS team.

## ID cards

### What's on each card

- school name, with "Student ID" and the school year (for example 2026–27)
- a box for the student's photo
- name, class and roll number
- date of birth, if the school has it
- ID: the student's login username
- emergency contact: the parent's mobile and relation
- "Valid till" 31 March at the end of the school year
- a space for the principal's signature

Photos aren't stored in Vidyalaya OS. Paste a passport-size photo in the box after printing.

### Print ID cards

1. Go to **ID cards**.
2. Pick the **Section**. Cards appear for every current student in it.
3. Click **Print**.
4. In the print window, choose A4 paper and set the scale to **100%** (not "Fit to page").
5. Print, then cut along the dashed lines.

The cards are real ID-card size (85.6 × 54 mm, the size of a bank card), ten to an A4 sheet. They print clearly even if "Background graphics" is off in your browser's print settings.

Each type has its own running number for your school: the first TC is 1, the next is 2, and so on. The middle part shows the school year it was issued in.

### Issue a certificate

1. Go to **Certificates** and click **Issue certificate**.
2. Choose the **Certificate** type.
3. Find the student by name, username or roll number, and select them.
4. For a **bonafide** certificate, optionally fill in **Needed for** (for example "Passport application").
5. For a **transfer** certificate, fill in the **Reason for leaving** and **Conduct** (it starts as "Good").
6. Click **Issue & print**.

The certificate opens, ready to print. Click **Print**.

### What the certificate says

- **Bonafide:** the student's name, parent's name (if a parent is linked), class and roll number, the school year, date of birth if known, and the purpose.
- **Transfer:** the same details, the date they left, the admission date, reason for leaving and conduct.

Both have the certificate number, date, "Prepared by" with the name of the person who issued it, and a line for the principal's signature.

### A TC marks the student as left

Issuing a transfer certificate **marks the student as left** from that day, if they weren't already. Their login is turned off and they come off the bus and hostel. Their records stay. See [Year end and student changes](/docs/year-end).

### Finding certificates later

The **Certificates** page lists the certificates you've issued, newest first, with a **View** link to reprint it. Certificates also appear on the student's profile page.

## Tips

- Sign and stamp certificates by hand after printing.
- Issue a TC only when the student is really leaving; it ends their login.
