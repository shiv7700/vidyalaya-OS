The timetable has two parts: the **bell timings**, which are the same for the whole school, and each **section's weekly grid** from Monday to Saturday. The school admin sets up both.

The timetable does more than show who is where. It also decides which subject teachers can enter marks and set homework for a section.

## Before you start

- Classes and sections exist. See [Classes, sections and teachers](/docs/classes-and-teachers).
- Each section's class teacher has added its **subjects** under **My class**. The timetable only offers subjects that the section has.
- Teachers have been added.

## Step 1: Set the bell timings

1. Go to **Timetable** (under Academics) and click **Bell timings**.
2. For **Period 1**, set when it **Starts** and **Ends**.
3. Click **Add period** for each further period. A new period starts when the last one ends and lasts just as long; change the times as needed. You can have up to **12 periods**.
4. Click **Remove last** to drop the last period.
5. Click **Save timings**.

Rules:

- Each period must end after it starts.
- A period can't start before the one before it ends. Leave a gap between periods for short breaks or lunch.
- You can't remove a period that still has classes in any section's timetable. Clear those periods first.

## Step 2: Fill in a section's week

1. On the **Timetable** page, pick a **Section**.
2. Click any empty cell in the grid (a day and a period).
3. Choose the **Subject** and the **Teacher**. "No teacher" is allowed.
4. Click **Save**.

To change a period, click it again. To empty it, click **Clear period**.

## No clashes

A teacher can't be in two places at once. If you pick a teacher who already has a class in that period on that day, the app refuses and tells you where they are, for example "Mrs Rao already teaches 6-B in this period". Pick another teacher or move one of the periods.

## What the timetable unlocks

When a teacher is placed on a section's timetable for a subject:

- they can **enter marks** for that subject in that section ([Exams and report cards](/docs/exams))
- they can **set homework** for that section ([A teacher's day](/docs/teachers))

A class teacher can always do both for their own section, timetable or not.

## When a teacher leaves

Turning a teacher off on the **Teachers** page takes them off the timetable. Their periods stay with the subject but no teacher, so you can put someone else in.

## Tips

- Do bell timings once, at the start of the year.
- Fill one class at a time, with its subject teachers' own timetables in mind.
- If a subject is missing from the list, ask that section's class teacher to add it under **My class**.
