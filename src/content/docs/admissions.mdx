The **Admissions** page keeps every enquiry in one place, from the first phone call to the day the child joins. When a family decides to join, one click creates the student's login and the parent's login.

## Add an enquiry

For walk-ins, phone calls and referrals:

1. Go to **Admissions** (under People) and click **Add enquiry**.
2. Enter the **Child's name**, and optionally the **Date of birth**.
3. Pick the **Class applying for**, or leave it as "Not sure yet".
4. Enter the **Parent's name** and a 10-digit **Mobile number**. **Email** is optional.
5. Choose **How they reached us**: Walk-in, Phone, Website form, Referral or Other.
6. Optionally set a **Follow up on** date and add **Notes**.
7. Click **Add enquiry**.

## The public enquiry form

The Admissions page shows a link to your school's **public enquiry form**. Put it on your website, share it on social media, or send it to families who ask.

- Parents don't need a login to use it.
- They fill in the child's name, date of birth (optional), class, parent's name, mobile, email (optional) and a note.
- After they click **Send enquiry**, they see a thank-you message, and the enquiry appears in your list as **New**, from **Website form**.

The link contains a random code, so people can't guess other schools' forms.

## Follow up

1. Click **Update** on an enquiry.
2. Change the **Status**, set the next **Follow up on** date and add to the **Notes**.
3. Fix any of the family's details if needed.
4. Click **Save**.

Follow-up dates that are today or earlier show in red. Your **Dashboard** also lists new enquiries and enquiries to follow up today under **Needs attention**. Web links in notes can be clicked.

## Admit a student

When the family confirms, click **Admit** on the enquiry.

1. Pick the **Section**. Sections of the class they applied for are listed first.
2. Enter a **Roll no** (1 to 10 letters or digits, free in that section).
3. Set the **Student's first password** (at least 8 characters).
4. Choose **Parent is the**: Mother, Father or Guardian.
5. Set the **Parent's password** (at least 8 characters). Leave it empty if this mobile number already has a parent login, for example for a sibling already at the school.
6. Click **Admit**.

Vidyalaya OS then:

- creates the student in that section, with a login
- creates a parent login for the mobile number, or links the existing one
- marks the enquiry **Admitted**, with the section and roll number

A message shows the new student username and the parent login. Share them with the family. Both will choose their own password when they first log in.

An admitted enquiry can't be admitted again, so double clicks don't create two students.

## Tips

- Log every call, even if the family seems unsure. Enquiries marked **Not joining** stay on record, and you can still update them later.
- Use follow-up dates for every open enquiry so nothing slips through.
- After admitting, the student appears under **Students** and in the class teacher's **My class**. From there you can bill fees, add them to a bus route and print an ID card.
