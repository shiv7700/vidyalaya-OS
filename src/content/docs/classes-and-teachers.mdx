This page is for school admins. It covers adding teachers, building your classes and sections, and choosing a class teacher for each section.

## Teachers

### Add a teacher

1. Go to **Teachers** in the sidebar (under People).
2. Click **Add teacher**.
3. Enter the teacher's **Name**, **Email** and a first **Password** (at least 8 characters).
4. Click **Add teacher**.

Share the email and password with the teacher. They log in with their email and must choose their own password the first time. Each email can be used only once.

### Edit a teacher

Click **Edit** on the teacher's row to fix their name or email. They log in with the new email from then on.

### Reset a teacher's password

If a teacher forgets their password, click **Reset password**, type a new one (at least 8 characters) and click **Set password**. Their old password stops working at once. Tell them the new one; they'll be asked to pick their own at next login.

### When a teacher leaves

Click **Turn off**. The teacher can no longer log in and comes off the timetable. Their records (attendance they marked, marks, leave, payslips) stay. You can click **Turn on** later if they come back.

You can't turn off a teacher who is still a class teacher. Assign another class teacher to their section first.

## Classes and sections

A **class** is a year group, such as "Class 5" or "Nursery". A **section** is a division of it, such as A or B. Students, subjects, attendance and the timetable all belong to a section, shown as "Class 5-A".

### Add a class

1. Go to **Classes** (under People).
2. Click **Add class**.
3. Type a name like "Class 5", "Nursery" or "UKG", and click **Add class**.

Class names must be different. "class 5" and "Class 5" count as the same name.

### Add sections

On the class, click **Add section**, type the section name (usually a letter, up to 10 characters) and click **Add section**.

### Rename or delete

- **Rename** works on both classes and sections. Renaming a section doesn't change student usernames; they keep the old name.
- A section can be deleted only if it has never had students and has no subjects. Its timetable and section notices are removed with it.
- A class can be deleted only when it has no sections left.

## Class teachers

Every section should have one class teacher. The class teacher:

- adds students, subjects and parent logins for the section under **My class**
- marks daily attendance
- enters marks for any subject of the section
- writes remarks on report cards
- resets passwords for their students and parents
- can post notices to their section's students and parents

### Assign a class teacher

On the **Classes** page, each section row has a **Class teacher** list. Pick a teacher and the change is saved straight away. Pick **None** to remove them.

A teacher can be class teacher of **only one section**. Teachers who already have a section appear in the list with "(class teacher of 6-B)" and can't be picked. Turned-off teachers don't appear.

Sections without a class teacher show up under **Needs attention** on your dashboard. Until one is assigned, you can still mark that section's attendance yourself. See [Attendance and holidays](/docs/attendance).

## Subject teachers

A teacher who teaches a subject in a section, but isn't its class teacher, is a subject teacher. You don't add them separately. Put them in that section's timetable, and they can then enter marks and set homework for those subjects. See [Timetable](/docs/timetable).

## Subjects

Subjects belong to a section and are added by its class teacher under **My class**. The admin chooses from these subjects when building the timetable. A subject that has marks, homework or timetable periods can't be removed.

## Moving students between sections

- One student: open them from **Students** and click **Move section**.
- A whole section at year end: click **Move all students** on the section row.

See [Year end and student changes](/docs/year-end).
