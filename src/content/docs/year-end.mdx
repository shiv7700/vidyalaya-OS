This page covers changes to students during the year and at year end: editing details, moving sections, promoting whole classes, and students who leave or come back. These are school admin tasks, apart from editing, which the class teacher can also do.

## The school year

Vidyalaya OS follows the Indian school year, **April to March** (for example 2026–27). Exams, fees, transport and hostel bills are filed under the school year automatically, so you don't have to start anything new on 1 April. Old records stay as they are.

Attendance can only be marked from 1 April of the current year. Remember to add the new year's holidays under **Holidays**.

## The student profile page

Go to **Students** and click a student's name. The profile brings everything together: attendance, fees due, class teacher, admission date, parents, bus and hostel, fees, exams (with report card links), library books and certificates. The top of the page has the buttons for the changes below.

The **Students** list can be filtered by section, searched by name, username or roll number, and switched between **Current students** and **Left the school**.

## Edit a student

Click **Edit** on the profile. You can change the **Name** and **Roll no**. The login username stays the same even if the roll number changes. Class teachers can also do this from **My class** by clicking the student's name.

## Move one student to another section

1. Click **Move section** on the profile.
2. Pick the **New section**.
3. Click **Move**.

The student's roll number must be free in the new section. Change it with **Edit** first if needed. Attendance, marks and fees already recorded stay with the old section.

## Promote a whole section (year end)

On the **Classes** page, click **Move all students** on a section's row, pick where they go (for example 5-A → 6-A) and click **Move students**.

- All current students in that section move together.
- Their roll numbers must be free in the new section. If any are taken, the app lists them and nothing moves.
- Past attendance, marks and fees stay as they are.
- Usernames don't change.

### Doing the whole school

Because roll numbers must be free, **work from the highest class downwards**:

1. Mark the outgoing class as left: on **Classes**, click **All left** on its section, check the last day and reason ("Passed out"), and confirm. Every current student in that section is marked as left at once.
2. Move Class 11 into Class 12, then Class 10 into Class 11, and so on.
3. Finish with the youngest class. New admissions then go into the empty entry class.

Roll numbers only need to be free among **current** students. Once the outgoing class is marked as left, their section is free for the next batch, and their records stay where they were. A new student can even get the same roll number as someone who left: their username gets a number added (for example `dps-5a-1-2`), because usernames never change once given.

## Mark a student as left

1. Click **Mark as left** on the profile.
2. Check the **Last day** (today or earlier).
3. Give a **Reason**, for example "Moved to another city".
4. Click **Mark as left**.

What happens:

- the student's login is turned off
- they come off their bus route and out of their hostel room
- they no longer appear in attendance sheets, marks, billing or ID cards
- all their records are kept

Issuing a **transfer certificate** marks the student as left too. See [ID cards and certificates](/docs/documents).

## Rejoin a student

Find them under **Students** → **Show** → **Left the school**, open their profile and click **Rejoin**. They come back to their old section and can log in again. Put them back on a bus or in the hostel if needed.

## Parents when a student leaves

Marking a student as left doesn't turn off the parent's login. The parent can still log in and see the child's past attendance, results, fees and receipts. A parent login stops working only when no children at all are linked to it.

## Teachers who leave

Go to **Teachers** and click **Turn off**. See [Classes, sections and teachers](/docs/classes-and-teachers).

## Year-end checklist

1. Finish the last exams and print report cards.
2. Collect or settle outstanding fees.
3. Issue TCs to students who are leaving.
4. Promote sections, highest class first.
5. Change class teachers on the **Classes** page if they rotate.
6. Add the new year's holidays.
7. Update the timetable if subjects or teachers change.
8. Print new ID cards; they show the new school year and validity.
