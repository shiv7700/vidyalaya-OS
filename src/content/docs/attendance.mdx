Attendance is taken once a day for each section, usually by the class teacher. The school admin sees a daily report for the whole school and can mark any section too.

The attendance percentage is present days (including late) out of all days marked, leaving out days on leave. In the admin's student list, a percentage below 75% is shown in red, because most boards ask for 75% to sit exams.

## Marking attendance (class teacher)

1. Click **Attendance** in the sidebar. Today's date is already picked.
2. Everyone starts as **Present**. Tap **Absent**, **Late** or **Leave** only for the students who need it.
3. Check the count at the top, for example "32 present · 2 absent".
4. Click **Save attendance**.

The badge changes from **Not saved** to **Saved**. If you try to leave the page before saving, the app warns you.

### Correcting a day

Pick an earlier date in the **Date** box. That day's saved attendance loads. Change what you need and click **Save changes**. You can correct any day in the current school year.

### Your Today page

Your **Today** page shows whether today's attendance is marked, with a **Mark attendance** link if it isn't.

## When attendance can't be marked

- **Sundays and school holidays.** The sheet says "School is closed" with the reason. Pick another date.
- **Before the current school year.** The school year runs April to March, so you can only go back to 1 April.
- **Future dates.** Only today and earlier.
- **Before a student joined.** Students added mid-year are skipped for days before they joined.
- **Students who have left** don't appear on the sheet.

## The daily report (school admin)

Go to **Attendance** under Academics. For the chosen date, each section shows:

- its class teacher (or "None — assign one")
- number of students, present and absent
- **Marked** or **Not marked**

Pick another date in the **Date** box to see earlier days. Your **Dashboard** shows today's totals, including how many sections haven't marked yet.

### Marking a section yourself

Click **Mark** (or **Open**, if it's already marked) on any section's row. You get the same sheet the class teacher uses. This is handy when a section has no class teacher, or the class teacher is on leave.

## Holidays

Go to **Holidays** under Academics (or the **Holidays** link on the Attendance page).

1. Pick the **Date**.
2. Type the name, for example "Diwali".
3. Click **Add holiday**.

On a holiday:

- attendance can't be marked
- the report says the school is closed
- teachers' leave over that day isn't counted as a leave day

Sundays are always off, so you don't need to add them. Click **Remove** to turn a holiday back into a school day. Add holidays one day at a time.

## Where families see attendance

Students and parents see attendance on the student's page, in the **Attendance** tab: the percentage, present and absent days, and a day-by-day list. If a child is marked absent today, the parent's home page shows a red banner saying so. See [Guide for parents and students](/docs/parents).

The student's profile page (for admins and the class teacher) and each report card also show attendance.

## Good habits

- Mark attendance at the same time every day, for example after the first period.
- Add the year's holidays at the start of the session.
- Check the dashboard for sections that are not marked before the end of the day.
