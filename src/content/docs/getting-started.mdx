Vidyalaya OS runs in your web browser, so there is nothing to install. This page walks you through a first set-up, in the order that works best. Most schools finish it in an afternoon.

## Before you start

Your school's account is set up by the Vidyalaya OS team. They create the school and a login for the school admin (usually the principal or office head). You will get an email address and a first password.

1. Open Vidyalaya OS and log in with that email and password.
2. The app asks you to **choose your own password** before anything else. Use at least 8 characters.
3. You land on the **Dashboard**.

If you haven't got a login yet, [contact us](/contact).

## Step 1: Add your school's details

Go to **School details** in the sidebar. Fill in the address, phone and email, then click **Save**. These are printed at the top of report cards, fee receipts, certificates and payslips.

## Step 2: Add teachers

Go to **Teachers** and click **Add teacher**. Enter the name, email and a first password. Share the email and password with the teacher. They will choose their own password when they first log in.

See [Classes, sections and teachers](/docs/classes-and-teachers) for more.

## Step 3: Create classes and sections

1. Go to **Classes** and click **Add class** (for example "Class 5", "Nursery" or "UKG").
2. Click **Add section** on that class (usually A, B, C).
3. In each section's row, pick a **Class teacher** from the list.

A teacher can be class teacher of only one section.

## Step 4: Add holidays

Go to **Holidays** (under Academics) and add the school's holidays for the year. Sundays are always off, so you don't need to add them. Attendance can't be marked on holidays.

## Step 5: Add students and parents

You have two ways:

- **Class teachers** open **My class** and use **Add student** or **Import from CSV**. They also add subjects and link parent logins here.
- **You, the admin,** can go to **Students** and click **Import from CSV** to add a whole section at once, parents included.

See [Importing students from Excel](/docs/importing-students).

## Step 6: Subjects and the timetable

Each class teacher adds their section's subjects under **My class**. Then you set bell timings and the weekly timetable. The timetable also decides which subject teachers can enter marks and set homework. See [Timetable](/docs/timetable).

## Step 7: Switch on what you need

Every module is ready to use. Start with the ones your school needs:

- [Attendance and holidays](/docs/attendance)
- [Fees, receipts and concessions](/docs/fees)
- [Exams and report cards](/docs/exams)
- [Admissions](/docs/admissions)
- [Leave and payroll](/docs/staff)
- [Library, transport and hostel](/docs/library-transport-hostel)
- [ID cards and certificates](/docs/documents)

## Your dashboard

The **Dashboard** shows today's attendance (students, present, absent, sections not marked) and a **Needs attention** list. It flags overdue fees, leave requests waiting for you, new admission enquiries, follow-ups due today, overdue library books and sections without a class teacher. Each line links to the page where you deal with it.

## Tips

- The sidebar groups (People, Documents, Academics and so on) can be folded to keep the menu short.
- Pick **Light**, **Dark** or **Match system** under **Theme** at the bottom of the sidebar.
- Every printable page (receipts, report cards, certificates, payslips, ID cards) has a **Print** button. You can also save it as a PDF from the print window.
