This guide is for teachers. It follows a normal school day: attendance, your class, homework, marks and notices, plus your own leave and payslips.

Log in with your email and the password the school office gave you. The first time, you'll be asked to choose your own password. See [Logging in and passwords](/docs/logging-in).

## Class teacher or subject teacher?

- A **class teacher** looks after one section (for example 5-A). The school admin assigns this under **Classes**. You get **My class**, attendance and more.
- A **subject teacher** teaches subjects in sections through the timetable. You can enter marks and set homework for those subjects.

Many teachers are both. If you're not a class teacher, **My class** and **Attendance** tell you so; ask your school admin if that's wrong.

## Your menu

**Today**, **My class**, **Attendance**, **Homework**, **Marks**, **Results**, **My timetable** and **Notices**, and under **Me**: **Leave** and **Payslips**.

## Start with Today

Your **Today** page shows:

- whether your class's attendance is marked, with a **Mark attendance** link
- quick links to **Set homework**, **Enter marks** and **Leave** (and how many leave requests are waiting)
- **Your periods today**, with times, subject and section
- the latest school notices

## Mark attendance

1. Click **Attendance**. Today is already picked.
2. Everyone starts as **Present**. Tap **Late**, **Absent** or **Leave** for the students who need it.
3. Click **Save attendance**.

To fix an earlier day, pick it in the **Date** box, change it and click **Save changes**. You can't mark Sundays, school holidays or days before this school year (1 April). More in [Attendance and holidays](/docs/attendance).

## Look after your class

In **My class** you can:

- **Add student**: name, roll number (1 to 10 letters or digits) and a first password of at least 8 characters. The login username appears as you type.
- **Import from CSV**: add many students at once. See [Importing students from Excel](/docs/importing-students).
- **Reset password** for a student who has forgotten theirs.
- **Parents**: add a parent with their name, relation and 10-digit mobile number, which is their login. Give a password for a new parent. Leave it empty if the mobile already has a login (a brother or sister is at the school); the existing login is linked. Both mother and father can have logins. From here you can also reset a parent's password or **Unlink** them.
- **Subjects**: type a subject, like "Maths", and click **Add**. Subjects are needed for marks, homework and the timetable. A subject with marks, homework or timetable periods can't be removed.

Click a student's name to open their profile: attendance, fees, results, library books, bus and hostel. Click **Edit** there to fix their name or roll number.

## Set homework

1. Click **Homework**.
2. Pick the **Section**. You'll see your own class and any section where the timetable gives you periods.
3. Click **Set homework**.
4. Choose the **Subject**, the **Due on** date (today or later), what to do (for example "Exercise 4.2") and any **Details**.
5. Click **Set homework**.

Students and parents see last week's homework and everything coming up on their page. You can edit or remove homework you set. Students don't submit homework in the app.

## Enter marks

1. Click **Marks**.
2. Pick the **Exam** (the school admin creates exams) and the **Subject**.
3. Set **Max marks**, type each student's marks (half marks like 42.5 are fine) or tick **Absent**.
4. Click **Save marks**.

You can leave rows blank and come back later. Class teachers can then open **Results** to see ranks, grades and pass or fail, add remarks and print report cards. Full details are in [Exams and report cards](/docs/exams).

## See your timetable

**My timetable** shows your teaching week, Monday to Saturday: the subject and section for each period.

## Post a notice to your class

Class teachers can click **Notices** → **Post notice**. Add a title and the notice. It goes to your class's students and parents. You can edit or remove your own notices. Web links in notices can be clicked.

**Notices** also shows what the school sends to everyone or to staff.

## Apply for leave

Click **Leave** → **Apply for leave**. Choose Casual, Sick, Earned or Unpaid; pick the dates and give a reason. Sundays and holidays aren't counted. You can cancel while it's pending. The school admin approves or rejects it. See [Leave and payroll](/docs/staff).

## See your payslips

**Payslips** lists your paid salary month by month. Click **View** to see the details and **Print**.
