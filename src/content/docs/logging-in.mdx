## Logging in

1. Open Vidyalaya OS.
2. Enter your email, username or mobile number.
3. Enter your password. Click the eye button if you want to see what you typed.
4. Click **Log in**.

## Choosing your own password the first time

When someone else has set your password (a new account, or a password reset), the app asks you to **choose your own password** before you can do anything else.

1. Enter the password you were given as the **Current password**.
2. Type a **New password** of at least 8 characters, twice.
3. Click **Change password**.

The new password must be different from the one you were given.

## Changing your password later

Click **Change password** at the bottom of the sidebar. Enter your current password, then the new one twice. When you change your password, you are logged out on your other devices.

## Forgot your password?

Nobody can see your password, but it can be replaced with a new one:

- **Teachers:** ask the school admin. They go to **Teachers**, find you, and click **Reset password**.
- **Students and parents:** ask the class teacher. In **My class**, they click **Reset password** on the student's row, or **Parents** → **Reset password** for a parent.
- **School admin:** contact the Vidyalaya OS team.

After a reset, the old password stops working and any open sessions end. You'll be asked to choose your own password when you next log in.

## Account locked?

After **10 wrong passwords**, the account is locked for **15 minutes**. Wait and try again, or ask for a password reset.

## Other messages you might see

- **"This account is turned off."** A teacher who has been turned off, or a student marked as left, can't log in. Contact the school office.
- **"No children are linked to this login."** A parent login works only while at least one child is linked. Ask the class teacher to link your child.
- **"Your session ended."** You were logged out (for example, your password changed, or you logged out elsewhere). Log in again, and you'll return to the page you were on.

## Staying logged in and logging out

You stay logged in on a device for up to 7 days. Click **Log out** at the bottom of the sidebar when you're done, especially on a shared computer. Logging out signs you out on all your devices.

## Keeping accounts safe

- Don't share passwords, even with other staff.
- Parents: siblings share one login, so one mobile number and password covers all your children at the school.
- Your session is stored in a secure cookie, not in the page, and the app never shows your password to anyone.
