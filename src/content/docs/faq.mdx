Short answers to the questions schools ask most. Each one links to the full guide.

## General

### Do we need to install anything?

No. Vidyalaya OS runs in a web browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Open it, log in, and you're in.

### Is there a mobile app?

There's no app to download from an app store. Parents, students and staff use Vidyalaya OS in the browser. On a small screen, the menu folds into a **Menu** button at the top.

### How do we get started?

The Vidyalaya OS team sets up your school and your admin login. Then follow [Getting started](/docs/getting-started). To book a demo, [contact us](/contact).

### Can other schools see our data?

No. Each school sees only its own data. Inside a school, people see only what their role allows. A parent sees only their own linked children.

### Does it have dark mode?

Yes. Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Match system** under **Theme** at the bottom of the menu. Printed pages always come out light.

## Logins and passwords

### How do parents log in?

With their 10-digit mobile number and a password. The class teacher (or the office, through admissions or a CSV import) creates the login. Siblings share one parent login. See [Logging in and passwords](/docs/logging-in).

### Someone forgot their password. What now?

Teachers ask the school admin (**Teachers** → **Reset password**). Students and parents ask the class teacher (**My class**). The school admin contacts the Vidyalaya OS team. After a reset, the person chooses their own password at next login.

### Why is an account locked?

After 10 wrong passwords, the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask for a password reset.

## Messages and payments

### Does Vidyalaya OS send SMS or WhatsApp messages?

No. Notices are shown inside the app. Parents see absences, fees due, homework and notices when they log in.

### Can parents pay fees online?

No. Families pay at the school as they do now, and the office records each payment and prints a receipt. See [Fees, receipts and concessions](/docs/fees).

### We recorded a payment by mistake. Can we delete it?

Payments aren't deleted. Go to **Fee collections**, click **Void** and give a reason. The receipt shows VOID and the fee becomes due again.

### How do we charge a late fine?

There's no automatic fine. Bill the fine to that student using **Bill students** → **One student (e.g. a fine)**.

## Attendance

### Why can't I mark attendance for a date?

You can't mark Sundays, school holidays, future dates, or dates before 1 April of the current school year. See [Attendance and holidays](/docs/attendance).

### How is the attendance percentage worked out?

Present and Late count as present, Absent as absent, and Leave isn't counted. The percentage is present days out of the days counted.

### A section has no class teacher. Who marks attendance?

The school admin can. Go to **Attendance**, and click **Mark** on that section's row.

## Exams

### Can we enter half marks?

Yes, like 42.5. Tick **Absent** for a student who missed the paper; they show as AB.

### How are grades and pass or fail decided?

Grades follow a CBSE-style scale (A1 to E) on the overall percentage. A student fails if they score below 33% in any subject. See [Exams and report cards](/docs/exams).

### A subject teacher can't see their subject on the Marks page.

They need to be on that section's timetable for the subject. The school admin sets this under **Timetable**.

## Students and staff

### Can we add all our students at once?

Yes. Import a CSV file saved from Excel, up to 300 students per file, with parent logins. See [Importing students from Excel](/docs/importing-students).

### How do we promote students at the end of the year?

Use **Move all students** on each section in **Classes**, starting from the highest class. See [Year end and student changes](/docs/year-end).

### A student left. What should we do?

Open their profile and click **Mark as left**, or issue a transfer certificate, which does the same. Their login is turned off, and all their records are kept.

### A teacher left. Can we remove them?

Click **Turn off** on the **Teachers** page. They can't log in and come off the timetable, but their records stay.

### Are there leave quotas?

Not yet. The app counts the days each teacher takes of each type, and approved unpaid leave is deducted in payroll. See [Leave and payroll](/docs/staff).

## Things not built yet

### Can we upload student photos or our school logo?

Not yet. ID cards have a box to paste a photo after printing. Your school's name, address, phone and email are printed on report cards, receipts and certificates.

### Can students submit homework online?

No. Teachers set homework in the app and families see it, but work is handed in as usual.

### Can we export lists to Excel?

Not yet. Every printable page (receipts, report cards, certificates, payslips, ID cards, bus lists) can be printed or saved as a PDF from the print window.

### Are there library fines?

No. The app tracks due dates and shows overdue books, but doesn't charge fines.
