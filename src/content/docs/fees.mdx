The fees module is for the school office. You bill students, record the payments families make at the school, and print receipts. Families see what they owe on their own page.

Vidyalaya OS doesn't take online payments. Families pay the school the way they do today (cash, UPI, card, bank transfer or cheque), and you record each payment.

## Bill students

1. Go to **Fees** (under Finance) and click **Bill students**.
2. Under **Bill**, choose who to bill:
   - **All of** a class (every section of it)
   - one section, such as 5-A
   - **One student (e.g. a fine)**. Then find the student by name, username or roll number.
3. Type the **Fee name**, for example "Term 1 tuition".
4. Enter the **Amount per student (₹)**.
5. Pick the **Due date**. It can't be in the past.
6. Click **Bill students**.

You'll see how many students were billed.

### Rules

- A student is billed **once per fee name** in a school year. If you bill "Term 1 tuition" to a class again, students who already have it are skipped. You can use the same name again next school year.
- The amount must be more than ₹0 and at most **₹1 crore** per student. Paise are allowed, like 12000.50.
- Students who have left the school aren't billed.

### Reuse a fee from before

If you've billed fees before, the form shows **Bill a fee used before (optional)**. Pick one to fill in its name and amount, then change what you need.

## Find fees

The **Fees** page opens on fees that are **Due**. At the top you see totals for Billed, Collected, Outstanding and Overdue. Filter by **Section**, **Status** (All, Due, Overdue, Paid) or search by student or fee name. Overdue fees are marked in red.

## Record a payment

1. Find the fee and click **Record payment**.
2. The **Amount (₹)** is filled with the full balance. Change it for a part payment.
3. Choose **Paid by**: Cash, UPI, Card, Bank transfer or Cheque.
4. Check **Paid on** (it can't be a future date).
5. Add a **Note** if useful, such as a cheque number or UPI reference.
6. Click **Record payment**.

You can't record more than what's still due. Each payment gets the next **receipt number** for your school.

## Print a receipt

After saving, click **Print receipt**. The receipt shows your school's letterhead, receipt number, date, the amount in figures and in words, the student, the fee, how it was paid, the balance left and who received it. There's a line for the cashier's signature.

You can open any receipt later from **Fee collections** by clicking its receipt number. Students and parents can open and print their own receipts from the **Fees** tab on their page.

## Give a concession

1. Find the fee and click **Concession**.
2. Enter the amount to waive (up to what's still owed).
3. Type a **Reason**, such as "Sibling discount". It's required.
4. Click **Give concession**.

The concession and its reason show on the fee, on the student's profile and on the family's page.

## Fines and one-off charges

There's no automatic late fine. To charge a fine or any one-off amount, bill **One student** with a clear fee name, such as "Late fee – Term 1".

## Mistakes

- **Wrong fee billed:** click **Remove** on the fee. This is possible only while it has no payments and no concession.
- **Wrong payment recorded:** go to **Fee collections**, click **Void** on the payment and give a reason. The receipt number stays used, the receipt shows **VOID**, and the fee becomes due again. Payments are never deleted, so your records stay complete.

## Fee collections

**Fee collections** lists money received between two dates (this month by default). It shows the total collected, the number of receipts and a total for each payment method, which is handy for daily cash tallies. Voided payments are struck through and not counted.

## Transport and hostel fees

Bus and hostel fees are billed from the **Transport** and **Hostel** pages, one month at a time. They then appear here like any other fee. See [Library, transport and hostel](/docs/library-transport-hostel).

## What families see

On the student's page, the **Fees** tab lists each fee with its amount, due date, any concession, payments and receipt links. Money due, and whether it's overdue, also shows at the top of the page. See [Guide for parents and students](/docs/parents).
