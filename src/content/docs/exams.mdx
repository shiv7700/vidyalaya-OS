The school admin creates exams. Teachers enter marks, one subject at a time. Vidyalaya OS works out totals, percentages, grades, pass or fail and ranks, and makes a printable report card for every student.

## 1. Create an exam (school admin)

1. Go to **Exams** (under Academics) and click **Add exam**.
2. Type the **Exam name**, for example "Half-yearly 2026" or "Unit Test 1".
3. Optionally pick **Starts on**. This date decides which school year (April to March) the exam belongs to. Without a date, it goes into the current year.
4. Click **Add exam**.

Exam names must be different within a school year, so you can use "Half-yearly" again next year. Use **Edit** to rename or re-date an exam. **Remove** works only while no marks have been entered.

## 2. Enter marks (teachers)

Who can enter marks:

- the **class teacher**, for any subject of their own section
- a **subject teacher**, for the subjects the timetable gives them in any section

The admin doesn't enter marks.

1. Click **Marks** in the sidebar.
2. Pick the **Exam** and the **Subject** (shown with its section, like "6-A · Maths").
3. Set **Max marks** for this subject in this exam (1 to 1000).
4. Type each student's marks. Half marks are allowed, like 42.5.
5. Tick **Absent** for a student who missed the paper. They show as **AB**.
6. Click **Save marks**.

Rows you leave blank are skipped, so you can enter marks in more than one sitting. The page warns you if you try to leave with unsaved marks.

A mark can't be more than the max marks. If you lower the max marks below a mark that's already saved, the app refuses and tells you which mark is too high.

If the Subject list is empty, your section has no subjects yet (the class teacher adds them under **My class**), or you're not on the timetable for any subject.

## 3. See results

- **School admin:** **Exams** → **Results** on an exam, then pick a **Section**.
- **Class teacher:** **Results** in the sidebar, then open an exam to see your class.

The results table shows each student's rank, marks per subject, total, percentage, grade and result. At the top: how many students have marks, how many passed, the class average and the topper.

### How results are worked out

- **Percentage** is the total of all entered subjects out of their total max marks.
- **Pass or fail:** a student fails if they get below **33% in any subject**. AB counts as zero.
- **Rank** is by percentage. Students with the same percentage share a rank, and the next rank is skipped (90%, 80%, 80%, 70% gives ranks 1, 2, 2, 4).
- **Grade** follows the CBSE-style scale below, based on the overall percentage.

## 4. Add remarks

In the results table, click **Add remark** (or **Edit remark**) for a student. Type a short comment, up to 500 characters, and click **Save**. It's printed on the report card under "Class teacher's remarks". The class teacher or the school admin can add remarks. Save an empty remark to remove it.

## 5. Print report cards

Click **Report card** on a student's row (it appears once the student has marks). Then click **Print**.

The report card has:

- your school's letterhead, the exam name and date
- the student's name, class, roll number and class teacher
- every subject of the section, with marks such as "18/25", "AB", or "Not entered"
- total, percentage, grade, rank (for example "3 of 40"), attendance and result
- the class teacher's remarks
- signature lines for the class teacher, principal and parent
- the grade scale, printed at the bottom

Report cards fit one A4 page. To keep a copy, choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.

## What students and parents see

Students and parents open the **Results** tab on the student's page. It lists every exam the student has marks in, with a **View** link to the same report card, which they can print. See [Guide for parents and students](/docs/parents).

## Tips

- Create exams before teachers need them; teachers can't create exams themselves.
- Make sure subject teachers are on the timetable before marks entry starts.
- Agree on max marks per subject in advance, so all sections of a class match.
