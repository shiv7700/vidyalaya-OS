This guide is for parents and students. Vidyalaya OS shows you what's happening at school: attendance, homework, results, fees, library books, the bus and the timetable. Everything here is view-only; to change anything, contact the school.

## Logging in

- **Parents** log in with their **10-digit mobile number**.
- **Students** log in with their **username**, for example dps-5a-12. It's printed on the student's ID card.

Your first password comes from the class teacher or the school office. The first time you log in, you'll be asked to choose your own password of at least 8 characters.

Forgot your password? Ask the class teacher to set a new one. More help in [Logging in and passwords](/docs/logging-in).

Vidyalaya OS runs in your web browser, so there's no app to download. On a phone, tap **Menu** at the top to see the menu.

## For parents: My children

After you log in, **My children** shows a card for each child linked to your mobile number. Brothers and sisters at the same school all appear under one login. Both parents can have their own login.

Each card shows:

- today's attendance, with a red note if your child was marked **absent today**
- the attendance percentage
- homework due soon
- the latest exam results
- fees due, or "No fees due"

Click a card to open your child's page. Use **Class timetable** at the top to see their weekly timetable.

If no children appear, ask the class teacher to link your child to your mobile number.

## The student's page

Students see this page when they log in. Parents see the same page for each child.

### Needs attention

At the top, coloured tags point out what matters now, for example:

- **Absent today**, **Late today** or **Present today**
- homework due by tomorrow
- fees due, and whether they're overdue
- library books due in the next 3 days, or overdue

## Understanding attendance

- **Present** and **Late** both count as present.
- **Absent** counts as absent.
- **Leave** (with permission) isn't counted either way.

The percentage is present days out of all days counted. Many boards ask for at least 75%.

## Report cards

Open **Results** and click **View** next to an exam. The report card shows marks for each subject, the total, percentage, grade, rank, attendance, result and the class teacher's remarks. Click **Print** to print it, or choose "Save as PDF" in the print window to keep a copy. See [Exams and report cards](/docs/exams) for how grades work.

## Fees and receipts

The **Fees** tab lists what the school has billed and what has been paid. Click **Receipt** next to a payment to view or print it.

Fees are paid at the school, by cash, UPI, card, bank transfer or cheque, as your school allows. Online payment isn't available in Vidyalaya OS. Once the office records your payment, it shows here with its receipt.

## Homework

Homework set by teachers shows on the **Overview** tab: the subject, what to do, and the due date. Homework is done in notebooks as usual; there's no online submission.

## Notices

**Notices** in the menu shows announcements from the school and from your child's class teacher. The latest ones also appear on your home page. Web links in notices can be clicked.

## Timetable

Students: click **Timetable** in the menu. Parents: open your child's page and click **Class timetable**. It shows the week from Monday to Saturday, with the subject and teacher for each period.

## Your account

- Click **Change password** at the bottom of the menu to change your password.
- Pick **Light**, **Dark** or **Match system** under **Theme**.
- Click **Log out** when you're done, especially on a shared phone or computer.

## Need something changed?

Wrong name, a missing child, a fee question or a bus change? Talk to the class teacher or the school office. They make changes in Vidyalaya OS, and you'll see them here.
